Allergens

My bakes contain gluten (wheat), eggs and milk, and the Coffee cake contains walnuts. I bake in a home kitchen where nuts and other allergens are also used, so I can't guarantee that any bake is completely free from them. If you or anyone you're baking for has an allergy, please tell me when you order and I'll talk you through the ingredients.
